# Blinkit Product Teardown

**Project type:** Independent product teardown for an Associate Product Manager portfolio  
**Status:** Portfolio documentation in progress  
**Scope:** Review of the customer journey across home, search, product selection, cart, checkout, order tracking, and delivery.

## Project overview

This teardown examines the quick-commerce customer journey and organizes observations into:
- Existing experience: what appears to work well in the journey.
- Potential friction: areas worth investigating through usability research or behavioural data.
- Product opportunities: hypotheses for improving clarity and ease of ordering.
- Prioritization: an Impact × Effort framework to structure opportunities.

## Product hypothesis to validate

**Delivery-promise clarity** is one potential area to investigate. This is a hypothesis, not a confirmed user pain point or a finding from Blinkit's internal data.

Validation would require direct user research and/or behavioural evidence. No experiment results or measured business impact are claimed.

## Methodology and limitations

- This is an independent portfolio exercise, not work commissioned by or affiliated with Blinkit.
- No Blinkit internal analytics, customer records, or confidential information are claimed.
- Observations and recommendations should be treated as hypotheses unless explicitly supported by cited public evidence or documented research.
- The detailed teardown artifact (slides/PDF and any supporting screenshots) has not yet been added to this repository.
