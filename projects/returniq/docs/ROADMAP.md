# ReturnIQ Product Roadmap

## P0 — Core workflow
- AI root-cause classification across five return categories
- Low-confidence human review and manual correction
- Return-driver prioritization by estimated financial impact
- Experiment tracking for mitigation interventions
- Structured return-data export
- Golden-set evaluation and regression checks

## P1 — Integrations and operations
- Ingest return events through Shopify and BigCommerce webhooks
- Notify suppliers when critical defect patterns exceed defined thresholds
- Improve monitoring of model quality and human correction rates

## P2 — Advanced capabilities
- Multimodal analysis of customer-submitted damage photos
- More granular cohort and SKU-level intervention measurement
- Additional safeguards and audit trails for AI-assisted decisions

## Sequencing principle
Prioritize data quality, operator trust, and measurable mitigation outcomes before expanding automation or multimodal scope.
